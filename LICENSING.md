## What AGPL-3.0 requires

The AGPL is a strong copyleft license. In short:

- You may use, modify, and redistribute the software freely.
- If you distribute a modified version, or **run a modified version
  as a network service that other users interact with**, you must
  make the corresponding source code of your modified version
  available to those users under the same AGPL-3.0 terms.
- This applies to the entire combined work, not just the parts you
  changed.

If those terms work for you, you don't need to do anything else.
Use the software under the AGPL.

holder. The commercial license grants the same rights without the
copyleft obligations, in exchange for a negotiated fee.
